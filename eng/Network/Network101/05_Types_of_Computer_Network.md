# Types of Computer Network

> Let's learn about LAN, MAN, and WAN

<br><br>

Networks are classified into 3 types based on **scale** and **management scope**: `LAN`, `MAN`, and `WAN`

- **LAN (Local Area Network)**
  - User's internal network
- **MAN (Metro Area Network)**
  - A network that connects and manages roughly a city-sized area
- **WAN (Wide Area Network)**
  - A network that connects distant LANs together

<br>

In the past, the technologies used in LAN, MAN, and WAN were all different, making it easy to distinguish them by the **protocols** and **transmission technologies** used

However, currently most have been unified with **Ethernet**, making it meaningless for users to distinguish by transmission technology, so LAN, MAN, and WAN are distinguished based on **management scope**

<br>

<br>

### LAN (Local Area Network)

- Refers to relatively **small-scale** networks like home networks and office networks
- It does not require long-distance communication, so networks connected with relatively simple devices like **switches** have been called LAN
- Various technologies are used for cost, reliability, and management purposes,
  - But currently most use **Ethernet**-based transmission technology
- Beyond transmission technology distinctions, LAN in terms of **management scope** can be defined as a network operating on **self-built lines** within buildings or land owned by the user
  - In the past, large-scale networks like those in factories or universities were separately classified, but recently this distinction has become meaningless

<br>

<br>

### WAN (Wide Area Network)

- Used to connect networks at **distant** locations
- Networks that **connect distant LANs** or provide **access to the internet** fall under WAN
- Since WAN is a network of a scope that generally cannot be built independently, most cases involve leasing lines from `telecommunications carriers`
  - It is a network **rented** from `telecommunications carriers` for remote connectivity
    - Used when communication to remote locations is needed beyond one's own land or buildings, with costs incurred **based on usage contracts**

<br><br>

### MAN (Metro Area Network)

- The concept of connecting a city spanning a range of several to tens of kilometers into a network
- Generally, when distinguishing city-level networks,
  - If the network is built based on **infrastructure already owned by the carrier**, it is classified as **WAN**,
  - If the network is built through **self-owned infrastructure**, it is classified as **MAN**
